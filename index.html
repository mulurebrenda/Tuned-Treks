Device Charging


Charging ports are available for both rear passengers.

If you can’t immediately locate a charging cable, please check beside the seat where the seat base meets the backrest.

For safety and to prevent unnecessary power flow when the ports are not in use, charging remains disabled by default.

Simply connect your device and let your Host know you’d like the charging ports activated.
